# Phase 10 Plan: Flexible Partial Audit Evaluation & Dynamic Facility Scoping

## Objective
Enable field auditors to submit partial audits for ACS evaluation when inspecting a single school, village, or specific sections, eliminating rigid 100% completion blocks while ensuring mathematical purity in deterministic ACS scoring.
