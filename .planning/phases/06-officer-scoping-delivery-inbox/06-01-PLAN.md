# Phase 6 Plan: Officer Scoping, Automatic Delivery & Jurisdiction ACS Inbox

## Objective
Implement multi-district officer scoping, atomic transactional delivery upon analysis completion, historical back-fill upon scope assignment, and a zero-PII Government Officer Portal with an ACS Inbox, read/acknowledgment tracking, and scoped result inspection.

### Task 2: Backend Entities, Repositories, Scoping & Delivery Engine
- JPA Entities:
  - Delivery (org.abhisaran.delivery.persistence.Delivery)
  - OfficerDistrict (org.abhisaran.officers.persistence.OfficerDistrict)
- Repositories:
  - DeliveryRepository
  - OfficerDistrictRepository
- Service & Atomic Delivery Integration:
  - In AnalysisService.analyseLocation(...):
    - In the same @Transactional method, automatically find all active officers assigned to the location's district.
    - Insert a Delivery record for each officer.
    - Guarantee: Zero half-written runs or orphaned deliveries (DEC-007).
  - OfficerScopingService:
    - Assign/unassign districts to officers.
    - Historical back-fill: when a district is assigned to an officer, create Delivery records for all existing completed AnalysisRun records in that district.
  - DeliveryService:
    - Fetch inbox deliveries for officer with filters (district, unread status).
    - Mark delivery as read.
    - Acknowledge delivery with optional notes.
    - Enforce strict jurisdictional bounds: officers can only access results within their assigned districts.
- DTOs & Controllers:
  - OfficerDeliveryController (/api/v1/me/inbox, /api/v1/me/deliveries/*, /api/v1/me/results/*).
  - AdminOfficerController (/api/v1/officers, /api/v1/officers/{id}/districts).
- Unit & Integration Tests:
  - OfficerDeliveryIntegrationTest.java:
    - Atomic delivery on analysis.
    - Multi-district scoping isolation (Ranchi officer cannot see Dhanbad).
    - Historical back-fill on new scope assignment.
    - Read & acknowledge workflow.

### Task 3: Frontend Officer Portal & Admin Scoping Interface
- Officer Portal (OfficerInboxScreen.tsx):
  - Automatically displayed when currentUser.role === 'OFFICER'.
  - Inbox summary cards (Total Delivered, Unread, Acknowledged).
  - District filter dropdown (scoped to officer's assigned districts).
  - Delivery table/cards:
    - Facility code (Zero PII - no registry names or contact info).
    - Facility type & location (District, Block, Panchayat).
    - ACS Score badge with alert band color.
    - Coverage % & provisional flag.
    - Delivered at, Read status, Acknowledged status.
  - Action to inspect full continuity analysis view.
  - "Acknowledge Receipt" modal with timestamp and notes.
- Admin Officer Management (OfficerManagementScreen.tsx):
  - Accessible to Admin from navigation / Admin Home.
  - Register new officer with login ID, designation, and temporary password.
  - Multi-select district assignments with live preview.
  - View officer active status, assigned districts, and delivery counts.
- Navigation & App.tsx Integration:
